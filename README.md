# Neuroscience and AI: a history of convergence and divergence, 1943–today

An interactive timeline of the two fields since cybernetics: one community, then cycles of divergence and reconvergence. Every event links to its primary source; the "Sources & further reading" panel lists the histories the framing rests on.

Live: https://serre-lab.github.io/neuroai-history/

Arrow keys step through the eras; scroll to zoom; click a dot to open the paper. `#era=N` (0–6) opens on a chapter.

Thomas Serre, Brown University, 2026. Corrections and omissions: open an issue.
